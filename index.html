Prediction Market Research: Netflix Top 10

Fall 2026 – Present
Independent research project

Can a simple statistical model forecast Netflix's weekly #1 movie better than a real-money prediction market? I built one to find out, and track it against Kalshi prices every week.

  • Model trained on five years of Netflix US weekly Top 10 data (2021–2026) that estimates each movie's chance of finishing #1.
  • Automated daily data pipeline, built with AI coding tools (Claude Code), that collects Netflix lists, Kalshi prices every 15 minutes, and Wikipedia page views.
  • Weekly paper-trading scorecard (no real money) comparing model accuracy against the market, including trading fees.
  • Tested extra factors (reviews, box office, star power, online buzz) on held-out data and recorded which ones helped.
